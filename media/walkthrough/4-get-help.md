# Where to get help

**AURA: Explain an R error.** Copy the message R printed in the terminal, run this command, and paste it. If the extension recognizes the message, it shows why it happened and what to do, step by step, with a link to the course page that covers it. If it does not, it copies a question you can paste into your AI tool. Record that exchange in your AI Use Log.

**AURA: Where to get help** lists the help pages for your course.

**Still stuck after 15 minutes?** Stop, take a screenshot of the whole message, and email your instructor or come to office hours. Setup problems are common and almost always quick to fix when someone can see the message.
